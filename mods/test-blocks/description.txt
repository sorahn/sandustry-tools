Test Blocks adds creative utility structures for testing, building, and experimentation:

- Infinite Elements — continuously creates a selected element without consuming resources.
- Built-in element picker with search, matter filters, color swatches, and remembered selections.
- Infinite Trash — rapidly removes elements across its full footprint.
- Heat — maintains a hot thermal buffer and shares heat with adjacent thermal relays.
- Chill — maintains a cold thermal buffer and shares heat with adjacent thermal relays.
- Thermal interaction — absorbs heat from nearby lava and cold from nearby snow.
- Power — provides a large local energy store and maintains the global energy pool.
- Multiple sprite themes: Sandustry Demo, Editor Extensions, and Colorful (Sir Monkz).
- All blocks are unlocked automatically in the Infinite Test Blocks category.
- Designed for creative testing, prototyping, automation experiments, and sandbox builds.
